## Weighted rubric

Score each criterion from `rubric.json` on 0–5, cite evidence, and calculate
the weighted score. A score of 80+ is not sufficient unless both trace hierarchy

## Decision

- **Adopt:** weighted score ≥80, trace hierarchy ≥4, redaction/boundary ≥4,
  successful synthetic ingestion/query, and an approved private retention plan.
- **Limited pilot:** weighted score 65–79 with redaction/boundary ≥4; list the
  bounded gap, owner, deadline, and a no-customer-content constraint.
- **Reject/defer:** score ≤64, weak redaction, missing hierarchy, or any evidence
  of raw prompt/tool/credential export.

## Current architecture caveat

Phoenix receives a true OTLP trace hierarchy in this evaluation. The existing
AgentFanout `LangfuseHttpSink` intentionally sends independent sanitized events,
not linked spans/traces. Do not describe Langfuse as having equivalent hierarchy
until a separate, security-reviewed trace adapter exists.
